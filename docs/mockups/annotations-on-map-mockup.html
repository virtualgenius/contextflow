What this prototype is testing

Where. Annotations attach to a relationship as readily as to a context. On an edge they sit at the label point, offset to the side so the pattern label stays readable.

Kinds. Three problem severities plus two new kinds: Question (open, needs an answer) and Working well (a strength worth protecting). Severity only applies to problems.

Styles. Badge is today's counter pill. Sticky notes are today's labels made more sticky-like. Hotspots borrow EventStorming colors: pink for problems, green for strengths, purple for questions.

Filter. Kind chips plus a target filter. A single annotation list in the sidebar is the payoff of moving annotations to a top-level collection.
