Annotations

One list across the whole map. Click a row to spotlight it on the canvas. Drag a sticky to pin it; drag a context and its notes follow. Double-click a sticky to return it to auto layout.

What this prototype is testing

Where. Annotations attach to a relationship as readily as to a context. On an edge they sit at the label point, offset to the side so the pattern label stays readable.

Kinds. Three problem severities plus two new kinds: Question (open, needs an answer) and Working well (a strength worth protecting). Severity only applies to problems.

Styles. Badge is today's counter pill. Hotspots borrow EventStorming paper: pink for problems, green for strengths, purple for questions, yellow for info, with a slight tilt and shadow. Straight is the same paper without the tilt. Pastel paper is lighter paper with one dark ink. Card + color bar is a neutral card with a kind-colored edge. Tinted is today's label overlay for comparison.

Position. A pinned note stores an offset from its anchor (context center, or relationship label point) in canvas units. The target moving moves the note. A dashed leader appears once the note sits away from its anchor. The small dot in the corner marks a pinned note.

Filter. Kind chips plus a target filter. A single annotation list in the sidebar is the payoff of moving annotations to a top-level collection.
